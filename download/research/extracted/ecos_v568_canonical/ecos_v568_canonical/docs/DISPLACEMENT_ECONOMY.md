**Why?** Training models on their own outputs creates degrading feedback loops. Fresh, expert-verified
human judgment is the scarce input. The entire RLHF industry is built on this insight.

## What ECOS Does Differently

### 1. Dignity by Design
- Transparent, timely payment — published in the audit chain
- Ownership model — validators become stakeholders, not contractors
- Graduated progression — expertise earns higher rates, automatically

### 2. Validator Retention Math
Validator 40% share must clear the hourly threshold:
- Generalists: task pricing must support >=$ 18-25/hr take-home
- Domain experts: task pricing must support >=$50-95/hr take-home
- Rule: Price or batch tasks so 40% of fee clears the tier's hourly threshold.

### 3. Sybil Resistance from Day One
- Validator onboarding requires verified domain expertise signals
- Golden tasks (known-answer quality checks) embedded in validator queues
- Reputation score (0-100) gates task volume and tier access

### 4. No Sub-Threshold Wages
- Rule: never pay below local living wage equivalent
- ManualRail + BankTransferRail: fiat payout anywhere without Stripe dependency
- Israel-first BankTransferRail as template for Global South expansion

## The Three-Way Router as Dignity Engine

```
confidence >= 0.70  ->  AUTO_FLOW      (no human needed; respects everyone's time)
confidence 0.50-0.70  ->  BEGINNER     (AI-scaffolded; entry-level work)
confidence < 0.50     ->  SENIOR       (expert work; full tooling; quorum option)
```

- Beginners are never thrown into deep water — AI scaffolding supports them.
- Experts are never asked to do trivial work — routing preserves their time and rates.
- Everyone earns proportional to the difficulty they handle.
