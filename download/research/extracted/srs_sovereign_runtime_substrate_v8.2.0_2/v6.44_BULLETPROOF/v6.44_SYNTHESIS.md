### Agent F1: Formal Primitive Definition

**Mission**: Replace v6.42's circular "primitive" definition with a formal mathematical object.

**Result**: Chose **Candidate A (Complexity-Class Definition)**. A primitive is a pair Π = (C_Π, ρ_Π) where C_Π is a complexity class and ρ_Π is its canonical resource bound. The 5 v6.42 primitives instantiate as:

| Primitive | Complexity Class | Resource Bound |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|
| E (Execution) | P | Time |
| C (Compression) | L = DSPACE(log n) | Space |
| S (Scheduling) | NC | Depth |
| A (Adaptivity) | P^NP[log] = Θ₂^P | Adaptive queries |
| V (Verification) | NP = PCP[O(log n), O(1)] | Proof length |

**Key finding**: The 5 primitives are NOT mutually irreducible as v6.42 claimed. They form a **partial order**:
- L, NC ⊆ P ⊆ NP
- P ⊆ Θ₂^P

The v6.42 "5 × 5 mutual irreducibility matrix" decomposes under formal analysis into:
- **9 [PROVEN] inclusions** (the opposite of irreducibility)
- **11 [UNKNOWN] separations** (requiring open conjectures)
- **0 [PROVEN] separations**

**Verdict**: v6.42's mutual irreducibility claim is mathematically unsupported. To prove it would require resolving P ≠ NP, P ≠ NC, P ≠ L, NP ≠ coNP, and L vs NC — all open.
