# Roadmap

## Stage 0: Canon and language gate — completed

Canonical authority, coverage audit, source and claims registry, task graph, context
pack tooling, and English-only release policy.

## Stage 1: Baselines and product focus

Start with: EVAL-001, THR-001, DAT-001, IAM-001, MOD-001, SKL-001, OPS-001, LEG-001, COMP-001, PROD-001, RES-002.

Deliver product outcomes, evaluation, threat model, data lifecycle, identity, AI asset
registry, skills import, legal checklist, competitor evidence, research portfolio, and
sovereign backup foundations.

## Stage 2: Durable and governed core

Durable execution, resumable approvals, capability broker, secrets, memory, knowledge,
context, model qualification, and telemetry.

## Stage 3: Intelligence and interoperability

Adaptive reasoning, planner-builder-verifier execution, multi-agent coordination, MCP,
A2A, retrieval adapters, and policy adapter evaluation.

## Stage 4: Scale and optimization

Workers, deployment profiles, fault injection, capacity, cost, energy, synchronization,
risk-tiered isolation, plugin SDK, and optional remote burst.

## Guardrail

The roadmap is dependency-driven, not calendar-driven. A stage cannot bypass missing
evidence merely because a technology is popular.
