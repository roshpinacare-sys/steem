# Economics, Performance, and Energy

Optimization is multi-objective and quality-constrained. Minimize latency, cost, and
energy only after the workflow meets correctness, safety, privacy, and reliability thresholds.

## Cost model

Include hardware amortization, electricity, storage, bandwidth, remote usage, operator
setup, maintenance, incident response, backup, and migration. A no-license-cost component
is not a zero-cost system.

## Capacity model

Measure arrival rate, service time, concurrency, queue delay, saturation, memory pressure,
model loading, batch behavior, and overload degradation for named workflows.

## Energy method

Prefer measured AC wall energy for the complete system during a defined workload. When
unavailable, label software or hardware counters as proxies and disclose what they omit.
Report idle overhead, workload duration, quality, latency, throughput, hardware, model,
quantization, and thermal behavior together.
