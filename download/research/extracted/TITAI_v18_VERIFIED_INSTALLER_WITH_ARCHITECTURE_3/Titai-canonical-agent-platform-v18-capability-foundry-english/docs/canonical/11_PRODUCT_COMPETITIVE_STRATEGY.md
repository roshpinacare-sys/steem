# Product and Competitive Strategy

## Comparison unit

Compare complete user workflows, not screenshots or feature checklists. Measure setup,
completion, quality, control, privacy, recovery, integration, operator effort, latency,
and total cost.

## Candidate advantage pillars

- Local and offline authority.
- Durable, inspectable, reversible execution.
- Governed memory and knowledge with deletion and provenance.
- Explicit permissions and risk-aware human control.
- Domain workflow packs with measurable outcomes.
- Replaceable models, stores, protocols, and infrastructure.
- Continuous evidence-driven improvement without silent self-modification.

## Strategic discipline

Select at most three initial beachhead workflows. Reject features that do not improve a
selected outcome, reduce a critical risk, or create a reusable platform primitive. Keep
competitor claims dated and evidence-backed because products change rapidly.
