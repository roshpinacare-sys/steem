# TFA Evolution Studio v20.0.0 — Complete Autonomous Engineering Platform

**Release Date:** 2026-06-25
**Version:** 20.0.0
**Previous Version:** 19.0.0
**Bump:** major

This release completes the autonomous engineering platform by filling every
missing module, route, and UI surface identified during the v19 skeleton audit.
The platform now ships with a fully functional compiler, governance, intelligence,
architecture analysis, learning, memory, and automation subsystems.

---

## What is new

- **30+ lib modules** created across compiler, control-plane, architecture,
  learning, memory, intelligence, integrations, automation, governance, and agents.
- **Pluggable adapter ecosystem** with Python, Node.js, Next.js, and Static adapters.
- **Compiler orchestrator** with retry, timeout, cancellation, progress tracking,
  and concurrent job limits.
- **Evidence chain** extended with V11–V14 nodes (version coherence, strategy,
  architecture graph, reality checks, playbook updates, SLA, circuit breaker, delta manifest).
- **Release governor** with version reuse guards, workspace coherence, SHA-256
  integrity, and hard-blocking evidence gates.
- **Architecture intelligence** with import cycle detection, bottleneck detection,
  and duplication analysis.
- **Cross-project intelligence** for portfolio-wide pattern discovery.
- **Learning engine** with lesson capture, resolution, and backlog wiring.
- **Self-evolution** per-cycle evaluation with trend tracking.
- **Integration engine** with health checks, failover, and connector lifecycle.
- **Automation engine** with rule-based event processing and audit logging.
- **UI shell, card, badge** components plus all missing pages (compiler, integrations,
  automation, learning, intelligence, observability, governance, projects).
- **Circuit breaker** 3-state FSM (closed/open/half-open) with metrics export.
- **Metrics store** with trend analysis and bottleneck detection.
