# V33.30 — Controlled Human UX Pilot Instrumentation

V33.30 implements the first strategic-rebalance priority from V33.29: a controlled comparative human study for Chat/Push governed action.

The implementation measures baseline and governed flows from signed raw events rather than self-reported summary numbers. Consent is pseudonymous and privacy-minimized; withdrawal excludes evidence. Participant, researcher, instrumentation, observer and verifier roles are separated. Passing thresholds are fixed at five participants, three scenarios, at least 70% median step reduction, at most one median approval, at most ten seconds median comprehension, and 100% task success, accessibility, comprehension correctness and evidence closure.

The local proof uses five synthetic contract-test participants. Their structural metrics are 73.3% median step reduction, one approval and six seconds comprehension, but the verdict is `HUMAN_UX_PILOT_REQUIRED`. External human participant count remains zero. Production authority and external effects remain zero.
