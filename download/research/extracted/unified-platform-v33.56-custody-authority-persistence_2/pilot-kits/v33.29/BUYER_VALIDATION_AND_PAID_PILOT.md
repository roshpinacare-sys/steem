# Buyer Validation and Paid Pilot

## Targets

- Fifteen structured buyer interviews.
- Five confirmations of an urgent problem with an identified budget owner.
- Three design partners willing to supply a real workflow and repository.
- Two paid pilots or paid discovery engagements.

## Required evidence

- Organization-deduplicated signed interview record.
- Problem urgency, current cost, decision owner and procurement path.
- Competitor or incumbent alternative.
- Pricing response and willingness-to-pay range.
- Signed design-partner or paid-pilot evidence.

Interest, compliments or synthetic interview records do not count toward G2.
