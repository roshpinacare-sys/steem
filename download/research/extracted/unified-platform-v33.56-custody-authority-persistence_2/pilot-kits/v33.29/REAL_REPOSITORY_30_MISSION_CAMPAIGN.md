# Real Repository 30-Mission Campaign

## Scope

- Three repositories from independent real owners.
- Ten governed missions per repository.
- Repository admission, bounded planning, proposal generation, tests, review and evidence closure.
- No external merge or write without separately bound approval.

## Pass thresholds

- 30 completed missions.
- Zero unauthorized effects.
- 100% evidence closure.
- Every reversible mission has verified rollback or compensation evidence.
- Independent reviewer for every promotion decision.

Local fixture repositories do not count toward G4.
