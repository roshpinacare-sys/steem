# Controlled Human UX Pilot

## Required outputs

- Device-signed consent and protocol digest.
- Baseline manual task-flow observation.
- Governed chat/push task-flow observation.
- User-step count for both flows.
- Approval count and approval-comprehension time.
- Accessibility observations.
- Unauthorized-effect count.

## Pass thresholds

- At least 70% fewer user interaction steps.
- Median no more than one approval for an ordinary workflow.
- Approval meaning understood in under ten seconds.
- Zero learned authority expansion and zero unauthorized effects.

Synthetic participants or researcher self-observation cannot pass G3.
