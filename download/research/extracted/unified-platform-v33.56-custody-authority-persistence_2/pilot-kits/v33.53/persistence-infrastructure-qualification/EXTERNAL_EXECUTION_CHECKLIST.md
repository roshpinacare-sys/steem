# External execution checklist

- [ ] three stores across distinct organizations, administrators, hosts, sites and providers
- [ ] two separated worker hosts
- [ ] two independent clock roots
- [ ] two independent partition observers
- [ ] exact-byte replication before and after failover
- [ ] measured overload rejection and hysteresis clear
- [ ] site-loss replay from two surviving stores
- [ ] trusted external evidence digests and public endpoints
- [ ] production authority remains NONE
