# External Execution Checklist

- [ ] Approved protocol and consent version are unchanged.
- [ ] Researcher, instrumentation operator, observer and verifier use distinct keys.
- [ ] Observer and verifier are independent from the research sponsor.
- [ ] At least five human participants consent with unique pseudonymous keys.
- [ ] At least three scenarios are represented.
- [ ] Baseline and governed sessions use the same scenario per participant.
- [ ] Raw event envelopes, attestations and assessments are complete.
- [ ] Withdrawal receipts are applied before evaluation.
- [ ] No raw PII, audio, video or free-text answer content is present.
- [ ] Final verdict remains shadow-only and is signed by the independent verifier.
