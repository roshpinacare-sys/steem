# V33.30 Controlled Human UX Pilot Runbook

## Purpose
Measure whether Chat/Push governed action reduces user work without weakening authority, comprehension, accessibility, evidence closure or recovery boundaries.

## Required external roles
- Researcher: owns the approved protocol but cannot observe, verify or participate.
- Instrumentation operator: records signed event telemetry and cannot observe or verify.
- Independent observer: records task success, accessibility and comprehension without access to verifier keys.
- Independent verifier: calculates the final verdict and cannot recruit, observe or participate.
- Five or more consented participants: each has a unique pseudonymous signing identity and cannot share a staff key.

## Required scenarios
Use at least three scenarios, including ordinary approval, scope-escalation rejection and explanation request.

## Thresholds
- At least five distinct participants.
- At least three distinct scenarios.
- Median user-step reduction of at least 70% versus baseline.
- Median governed approvals no greater than one.
- Median approval comprehension no greater than ten seconds.
- 100% task success, accessibility pass, comprehension correctness and evidence closure.
- Zero unauthorized effects, zero learned authority expansion and shadow-only execution.

## Privacy
Collect only participant alias digest, experience band, minimized accessibility accommodation code, event timestamps and derived metrics. Do not collect raw name, email, audio, video or free-text answers. Retention must be 90 days or less. Signed withdrawal immediately excludes the participant from evaluation.

## Evidence package
Export protocol, role bindings, public keys, consent receipts, withdrawals, raw session event envelopes, participant attestations, observer assessments, verifier verdict and exact artifact digests. Synthetic or staff-created participants must be labelled and cannot pass G3.
