# Controlled Human Pilot Runbook — V33.7

1. Assign separate study-authority and independent-observer signing identities.
2. Freeze and sign one protocol before participant enrollment.
3. Enroll at least five participants using separate registered devices.
4. Show the consent text and store only its digest plus pseudonymous participant alias.
5. Run all fixed scenarios. Do not change thresholds after seeing results.
6. Record digest-chained telemetry and signed independent observations.
7. Reject revoked sessions, duplicate devices, duplicate aliases and missing scenarios.
8. Evaluate the gate without manual overrides.
9. Preserve raw evidence outside the repository under the declared retention policy.
10. Do not promote production even if G3 passes; external trust qualification remains separate.
