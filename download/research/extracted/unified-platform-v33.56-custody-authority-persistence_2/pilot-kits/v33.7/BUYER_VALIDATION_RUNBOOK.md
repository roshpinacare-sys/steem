# Buyer Validation Runbook — V33.7

Target roles: CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Platform, CISO, AppSec leader and regulated-technology operations leader.

For every organization capture a signed evidence record containing:

- pseudonymous organization alias;
- buyer role;
- current workflow and workaround digest;
- whether the problem is urgent;
- whether a budget owner is confirmed;
- evidence type: interview, design partner or paid pilot;
- artifact digest for notes, letter or executed pilot agreement;
- ACV only for paid-pilot evidence.

G2 cannot pass until there are 15 unique interviews, five urgent funded confirmations, three distinct design partners, two distinct paid pilots and at least USD 50,000 combined committed pilot ACV.
