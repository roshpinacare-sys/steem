# V33.33 External Continuity Runbook

1. Pin the V33.32 policy, anchor and all current witness keys outside the platform owner.
2. Assign a separate rotation authority, next coordinator, importer, five next witnesses, transparency operator, three monitors and pin authority.
3. Verify every organization, host and key is distinct where required.
4. Issue one activation challenge and collect proof of possession from all next witnesses.
5. Obtain at least three current-witness votes and issue the rotation certificate.
6. Publish the plan and certificate to the signed transparency log.
7. Issue a quorum-backed coordinator lease for a bounded sequence range.
8. Obtain at least two monitor votes and publish a predecessor-linked pin outside platform control.
9. Re-run independent verification from pinned public keys and exact artifact bytes.
10. Abort on rollback, sequence gap, excessive replacement, missing activation, lease overlap, equivocation, split view or inaccessible prior pin.

A successful ceremony permits only a controlled external pilot. It never grants production or automatic external-mutation authority.
