# V33.41 External Provider Onboarding Runbook

1. Pin each provider registrar and attestation authority out of band.
2. Retain the exact root-certificate and adapter-module bytes before any verification.
3. Generate a unique device session nonce and execute the full conformance vector under an independently administered laboratory identity.
4. Preserve signed receipts for key generation, non-extractability, sign/verify, blocked export and session closure.
5. Publish a monotonic revocation snapshot before replacing a provider.
6. Collect three separated custodian approvals for the exact replacement plan.
7. Confirm the active provider set remains five members and at least three independent providers.
8. Publish the exact checkpoint bytes outside platform control.
9. Retrieve and fsync the bytes from three independently administered monitors.
10. Restore the exact bytes at a distinct site and provider without reset or fork.
11. Run the offline verifier and retain all original bytes and signed envelopes.

This runbook does not authorize production promotion or external mutation.
