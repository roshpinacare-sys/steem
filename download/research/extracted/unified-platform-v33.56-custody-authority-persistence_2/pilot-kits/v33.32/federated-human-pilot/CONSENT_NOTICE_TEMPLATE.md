# Participant notice template

This is a usability study of a governed software workflow. Participation is voluntary. The study records only the approved task events and observer assessment fields. It does not collect raw identity documents, free-form personal data, audio or video.

You may stop at any time without penalty. On withdrawal, new collection stops immediately and linked study data is deleted within the approved deletion SLA unless law requires otherwise. Contact details for withdrawal and privacy questions must be inserted by the independent pilot operator before use.
