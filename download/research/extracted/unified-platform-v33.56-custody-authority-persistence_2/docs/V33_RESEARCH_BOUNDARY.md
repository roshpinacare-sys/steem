# V33 Research Boundary

## Purpose

Research systems may discover, simulate, compare, compress, falsify and propose. They may not authorize, execute external effects, issue production credentials, select their own independent reviewers or promote their own outputs.

## Included laboratories

- SRS adaptive execution and falsification.
- S-AOS experiment and reproduction workflows.
- Model tournaments and optimization engines.
- Formal models, mutation campaigns and adversarial probes.

## Mandatory flow

`observation → candidate → isolated experiment → holdout/falsification → independent qualification → shadow → explicit promotion decision`

## Prohibited paths

- Research result directly becomes policy.
- Research service possesses production secrets or effect credentials.
- Candidate chooses or registers its own verifier.
- Simulation is relabeled as measured evidence.
- Shortcut enlarges authority or bypasses rollback.

## Import contract

A research output enters the canonical platform only as an evidence-bounded candidate containing tenant, source lineage, method, environment, limitations, expiry, drift conditions, falsification results and a declared production ceiling.
