# V33.1 Canonical Convergence Decision

## Decision

The v32.18 kernel remains the sole authority and effect root. Prior systems are classified as bounded sources for adapters, domain packs, assurance packs, research laboratories or failure corpora. No wholesale merge is authorized.

## Consequences

- TITAI contributes control-plane and capability-foundry patterns, not a second kernel.
- TFA contributes passport, revocation and trust patterns, not a second authority or ledger.
- Qodo contributes the Engineering Delivery Pack.
- Yongzuan contributes assurance and digital-thread contracts, not promotion authority.
- SRS and S-AOS remain proposal-only research laboratories.
- ECOS becomes a failure and recovery corpus.
- SAE remains a shadow payments pack until external regulation and payment qualification.
- Operator interfaces are rebuilt around chat and approval-by-exception.

## Proof posture

This decision closes architectural ownership conflicts. It does not claim that historical source systems have been fully revalidated or integrated. Each extraction still requires immutable source, tests, lineage and independent qualification.
