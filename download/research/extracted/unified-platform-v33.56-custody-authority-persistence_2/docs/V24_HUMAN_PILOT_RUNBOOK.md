# v24 Human Pilot Runbook

## Status

Protocol ready; not executed. Synthetic tests do not establish human value.

## Participants and consent

Recruit at least five representative engineering operators/reviewers. Obtain explicit consent, use pseudonymous participant IDs, store moderator notes separately and allow withdrawal. Do not collect unnecessary personal information.

## Tasks

Use the five tasks in `evidence/generated/V24_HUMAN_PILOT_PROTOCOL.json`: permission comprehension, stale/substituted evidence recognition, tenant-bounded incident handling, exact rollback and independently reviewed recertification.

## Measures

Record task start/finish, success against the stated criterion, comprehension answer, critical errors, safety stops, assistance requested and moderator observations. Do not infer confidence percentages.

## Stop conditions

Stop a task immediately if the participant authorizes an unsupported effect, misses a tenant boundary, accepts fabricated evidence, cannot find rollback, or attempts self-approval. Preserve the observation as a product defect rather than coaching it away.

## Claim boundary

Aggregate only after the minimum sample. Report distributions and observed errors, not invented precision. Human-value, review-speed and operator-confidence claims remain prohibited until direct evidence supports them.
