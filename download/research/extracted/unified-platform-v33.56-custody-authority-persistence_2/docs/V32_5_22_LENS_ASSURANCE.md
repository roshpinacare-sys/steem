# v32.5 — Twenty-Two-Lens Holistic Assurance

v32.5 retains the fourteen promotion-critical excellence axes and adds eight non-overlapping complementary metrics:

1. Maintainability and evolvability.
2. Observability and diagnosability.
3. Data integrity and lifecycle governance.
4. Dependency and upgrade resilience.
5. Compatibility and migration safety.
6. Privacy and data minimization.
7. Accessibility and human factors.
8. Cost and capacity predictability.

The core score remains authoritative for promotion. The complementary score broadens diagnosis and cannot cancel a blocker in security, human value, PostgreSQL, multi-host, HSM/KMS or independent review.

All local observations are explicitly capped. Human accessibility outcomes, legal privacy review, external advisory monitoring, cloud billing, multi-host capacity and production storage lifecycle execution remain external evidence requirements.
