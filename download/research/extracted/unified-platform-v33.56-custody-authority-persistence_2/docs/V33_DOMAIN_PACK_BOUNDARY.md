# V33 Domain Pack Boundary

## Doctrine

A domain pack supplies language, workflows, adapters and evidence requirements for a business domain. It does not own identity, authority, effect truth, promotion or the durable mission kernel.

## Required pack contents

- Ontology and version.
- Commands and events.
- Required capabilities and scopes.
- Domain policies that may only tighten kernel rules.
- Evidence requirements and limitations.
- Approval, rollback or reconciliation rules.
- Tests, migration and removal procedures.
- User surfaces and connector adapters.

## Kernel-reserved concerns

`authority.*`, `tenant.*`, `secret.*`, `release.*`, `kernel.*`, identity roots, effect receipts and production promotion remain unavailable to packs.

## Initial packs

1. Engineering Delivery.
2. Engineering Assurance.
3. Research Evidence.
4. Platform Operations.
5. Payments Safety — shadow only.

## Acceptance rule

A pack is admissible only when it can be installed, disabled, upgraded and removed without changing kernel authority contracts or leaving hidden state/effects behind.
