# V33 Convergence Constitution

**Status:** Accepted for execution planning and bounded implementation  
**Version:** 33.0.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Production promotion:** Blocked

## Mission

Build a provider-neutral, sovereign platform in which people express intent through chat, governed agents perform bounded work, external effects occur only under explicit authority, and every material action is independently verifiable, explainable, recoverable, and revocable.

The first product proof is **Governed Agentic Engineering**. The long-term platform is **Evidence-Carrying Autonomy**.

## Non-negotiable constitutional rules

1. **One canonical kernel.** The v32.18 authority, evidence, mission, effect, replay, recovery, and promotion contracts remain the only canonical control plane until replaced through an explicit migration proof.
2. **One root of authority.** No source system, model, agent, domain pack, verifier, or laboratory may grant itself production authority.
3. **One effect ledger.** External reality is represented by authoritative effect receipts, not by model narration or workflow intention.
4. **One evidence vocabulary.** Simulated, computed, measured, observed, and attested evidence remain distinct. An attestation proves signer and integrity, not truth.
5. **Independent verification.** The builder, proposer, collector, reviewer, and promotion authority must remain separable according to risk.
6. **Research cannot promote itself.** SRS, S-AOS, formal experiments, optimizers, and model tournaments are proposal-only until independently qualified.
7. **Domain packs do not own kernel authority.** Engineering, payments, research, operations, and future packs declare domain semantics but cannot request kernel-reserved scopes.
8. **Chat-first, approval-by-exception.** Users express goals in chat. Routine reversible actions execute under policy. Push approval is reserved for material exceptions, irreversible actions, uncertainty, cost thresholds, or authority expansion.
9. **No hidden autonomy.** The system may learn and propose a new automation policy, but cannot enlarge its own authority without an explicit user or organizational approval.
10. **Recovery is designed before execution.** Every material action must declare rollback, compensation, pause, safe-mode, or an explicit non-reversibility warning before authorization.
11. **AI-generated code receives no special trust.** It must pass the same or stricter provenance, license, security, testing, independent review, and maintainability gates as human-authored code.
12. **Claims are bounded by reproducible evidence.** No stage, capability, valuation thesis, production claim, or customer-value claim may be promoted without a named artifact and a reproducible verifier.
13. **Original source archives remain immutable.** Extraction occurs through bounded reimplementation, adapters, or candidate modules with preserved lineage.
14. **Product proof precedes platform breadth.** The engineering vertical slice must prove value, trust, UX, and economics before broad domain expansion.
15. **Kill criteria are mandatory.** Every major hypothesis must declare evidence that would invalidate or narrow it.

## Canonical product doctrine

The platform does not sell “more agents.” It sells the ability to let agents perform real work with less human effort while preserving authority, auditability, recovery, provider independence, and organizational control.

The product experience must converge on:

> Ask in chat. The system performs the work. It interrupts the user only when a meaningful decision or new authority is required.

## Architecture boundaries

- **Canonical kernel:** authority, identity, tenancy, policy, durable missions, capabilities, effect ownership, evidence, replay, recovery, promotion.
- **Operator plane:** chat, voice, web, mobile, push approval, expert evidence view, emergency stop.
- **Engineering pack:** repository analysis, planning, coding, testing, review packets, PR preparation, deployment proposal, rollback.
- **Assurance pack:** requirements, uncertainty, digital thread, certification candidates, recertification triggers.
- **Research labs:** SRS, S-AOS, tournaments, compression research, formal models, falsification and optimization.
- **Domain packs:** payments, research, platform operations, and later domains. All remain outside the kernel.
- **External qualification:** independent identity, KMS/HSM, PostgreSQL continuity, isolation, multi-host SLOs, human value, security, license and operational review.

## Initial production ceiling

Until external qualification is complete, the maximum permitted state is:

`LOCAL_OR_CONTROLLED_SHADOW` → `REVIEWED_PROPOSAL` → `REVOCABLE_LIMITED_PILOT`

Production mutation, live payments, self-promotion, untrusted-code execution, and unrestricted external effects remain blocked.
