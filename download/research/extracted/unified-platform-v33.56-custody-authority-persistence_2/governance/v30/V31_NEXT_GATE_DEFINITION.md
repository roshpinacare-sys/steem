# v31 Next Gate — External Model and Human Value Qualification

## Objective

Prove that the governed campaign creates measurable value when connected to a real advanced-model provider and used by real operators, while preserving all v30 safety and evidence constraints.

## Mandatory gates

1. External provider identity, exact model version, request/response receipt and cost/latency evidence.
2. Fixed adversarial benchmark across architecture, security, testing, product and operations tasks.
3. Comparative human pilot with consent and counterbalanced manual versus governed workflow.
4. PostgreSQL primary/standby, failover, claim fencing and PITR rehearsal.
5. Authorized KMS/HSM, key rotation and revocation during execution.
6. Independent isolation and penetration assessment.
7. Licensing and supply-chain review.
8. Multi-host SLO, recovery and evidence-growth measurement.

## Preservation constraints

Minimum 99% line, 96% branch and 98.5% function coverage; no surviving critical mutation; no source cycle; no self-approval; two independent signed result attestations; proposal-only provider qualification; zero external side effects; no human-value claim without measured pilot evidence.
