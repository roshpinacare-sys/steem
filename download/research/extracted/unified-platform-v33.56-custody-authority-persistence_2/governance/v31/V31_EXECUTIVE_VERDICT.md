# v31 Executive Verdict

## Verdict

**Accept continued Integrated Pilot work; reject any production or external-model qualification claim.**

v31 materially improves the system because it replaces several self-asserted claims with falsifiable and enforced contracts. The strongest result is not “AI sovereignty” as a slogan, but a concrete chain from selected evidence through provider disclosure, signed attestation, current credential revocation, explicit interaction accounting and credentialed independent review.

## Claims changed

- “Evidence-bound claim” was downgraded until the implementation required the cited evidence to exist in selected context and, for supported claims, in trusted selected context.
- “Idempotent model request” was downgraded until single-flight behavior was proven under concurrency and restart.
- “Provider timing is evidence” was rejected; platform timing is authoritative and provider timing is diagnostic.
- “Two reviewers are independent” was rejected; distinct credentials and distinct declared organizations are now required.
- “Zero external effects” was rejected as ambiguous. v31 records provider interactions separately and requires zero external mutations.
- “Signed provider output is trustworthy” remains rejected. Attestation proves identity, authorization and binding, not correctness.

## Qualification boundary

The external provider, durable distributed trust, PostgreSQL cluster, KMS/HSM, independent security review, licensing review, human pilot and production SLO/DR remain unqualified.
