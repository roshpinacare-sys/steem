# v30 Next Gate — External Qualification and Measured Human Value

## Objective

Determine whether the v29 governed automation loop produces measurable operator value under realistic conditions and whether its external runtime boundaries can be qualified.

## Required work

- PostgreSQL 16 primary/standby with RLS, outbox, pool-isolation, failover and PITR fault injection.
- Authorized KMS/HSM signing, rotation, revocation and external monotonic witness.
- Independent untrusted-code isolation and penetration assessment.
- Licensing review.
- At least five consented representative operators/reviewers completing the six-task comparative pilot.
- Measurement of task time, decision count, errors, evidence comprehension, overrides and recovery speed.
- Independent reproduction of the v29 portable proof.

## Preserve

- minimum 96% in-process branch coverage;
- authentic mutation markers;
- independent dual review;
- proposal-only automation and model output;
- zero automatic external effects;
- explicit distinction between modeled, measured-local and human-pilot evidence.

## Stop conditions

Stop or downgrade if tenant isolation fails, an external effect occurs without explicit reviewed authority, a signer/reviewer can approve itself, evidence can be replayed or substituted, a human-value claim uses modeled data, or external qualification requires weakening a proven security gate.
