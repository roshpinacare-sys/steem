# v26 Design Decisions — Persistent Closure Without Kernel Expansion

Version: 26.0
Owner role: Distinguished Systems Architect
Status: Implemented and exercised locally

## Decisions

1. Recertification cases must survive restart with digest-bound incident, plan, Digital Thread and completion material.
2. Agent approval must apply only to a canonical persisted proposal derived from the original request and the unchanged target file.
3. Domain Pack registration must preserve generator approval lineage, dual review, semantic versioning and predecessor linkage.
4. High and critical design claims must close implementation, integration, adversarial testing, mutation, recovery, operations, evidence and independent review before they may be reported as complete.

These capabilities are adapters and validation controls. They do not enlarge canonical authority or qualify production infrastructure.
