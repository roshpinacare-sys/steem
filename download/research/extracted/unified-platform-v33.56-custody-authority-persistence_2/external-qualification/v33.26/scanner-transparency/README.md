# V33.26 External Scanner Evidence and Feed Transparency Kit

This kit collects signed ClamAV and YARA receipts from independent hosts, binds exact supporting artifact bytes, submits feed and receipt digests to an append-only transparency log, and produces inclusion and prefix-consistency proofs for an independent verifier.

It contains no scanner binaries, signature databases, credentials, private keys, or live evidence. Synthetic or same-host evidence cannot qualify the external pilot subgate and can never promote production.
